# Verified mission facts

- Mission: SkyFall
- Operator: NASA
- Purpose: Survey Mars, map terrain, and search for subsurface ice.
- Platform: Three autonomous Mars helicopters.
- Deployment: Released mid-air during descent.
- Science: Terrain, weather, radiation, and subsurface ice.
- Radar depth: Approximately 0.5–3 meters below the surface.
- Launch: Late 2028.
- Mars arrival: Fall 2030, after a second Mars approach.
- Relevance: Subsurface ice could support future oxygen and rocket-fuel production.
